# SmartPark AI

## AI-Based Smart Parking Management System

SmartPark AI is an independently developed full-stack dissertation project for intelligent urban parking management.

The system combines:

- parking availability management
- parking reservations
- AI parking-demand prediction
- intelligent parking recommendations
- AI-assisted dynamic pricing
- digital wallet and payments
- EV charger status management
- reservation barcode and scanner
- live reservation timers
- reviews and notifications
- administrator management and analytics

## Research Question

> How can artificial intelligence be used to predict parking demand and recommend suitable parking locations in an urban smart parking management system?

## Technology Stack

### Frontend
Vue 3, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Vue Router, Axios, Chart.js, Leaflet, JsBarcode and ZXing.

### Backend
Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Pydantic, Alembic and PostgreSQL.

### Machine Learning
XGBoost, scikit-learn, Pandas, NumPy and Joblib.

### Testing
Pytest and Vitest.
